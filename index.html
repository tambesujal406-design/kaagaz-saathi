    Never share

    Four habits that stop most fraud

    Scammers copy the look of official sites and calls. These habits work even when the fake looks real.

    • Keep OTPs and PINs to yourselfNo official will ask for them on a call, SMS or WhatsApp.
    • Type the address yourselfUse digilocker.gov.in, myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in and incometax.gov.in. Skip links in messages and ads.
    • Share a masked AadhaarWhen a full number isn’t needed, share a copy that shows only the last four digits.
    • Lock your biometricsLock them in the myAadhaar portal when you’re not using them. Unlock only when needed.

    Something went wrong?

    The five problems people hit most, and what to try first.

    The OTP doesn’t arrive

    Wait two minutes before asking again, and check your signal. Make sure the number is the one registered with the service. If you changed numbers, update it at an Aadhaar centre first.

    My document isn’t showing in DigiLocker

    Confirm you picked the right issuer and that your details match their records exactly. Try again later, since issuer services sometimes go down.

    My name doesn’t match across documents

    Compare spelling, initials and word order. A mismatch usually means one record needs a correction at the issuing office.

    I forgot my DigiLocker PIN

    Use the forgot-PIN option on the sign-in screen and verify with an OTP to set a new one.

    My upload fails

    Check the file type and size the app accepts, and your connection. A smaller PDF or a clear JPG usually works.

    Words you’ll hear

    URI
    A unique address for one document in DigiLocker.
    VID
    A 16-digit number you can give instead of your Aadhaar number. You can generate a new one anytime.
    Masked Aadhaar
    A copy that shows only the last four digits.
    e-KYC
    Verifying identity digitally, with your consent, instead of paper copies.
    e-PAN
    A digitally signed PAN card in PDF form.
